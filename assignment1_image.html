2. Bao Cao Chuan Bi Dataset, Dataloader Va Augmentation

Pipeline dataset trong training/dataset.py ho tro doc du lieu tu thu muc va file .zip, sau do chia tap du lieu bang stratified split de giu phan bo lop on dinh. 

variant = "original" | "augmentation" | "combined"
records = collect_records(data_root, variant)
splits = split_records(records, val_size=0.1, test_size=0.1, seed=42)

Dataloader su dung cau hinh batch-size va worker tu file config. Phan augmentation duoc quan ly tai training/transforms.py, gom cac phep bien doi:

  • RandomHorizontalFlip (mac dinh 0.5)
  • RandomRotation (mac dinh 15 do)
  • ColorJitter (brightness/contrast/saturation/hue)
  • RandomResizedCrop (scale 0.8-1.0, ratio 0.9-1.1)
  • Normalize theo mean/std ImageNet

3. Bao Cao Xay Dung, Huan Luyen, Danh Gia Va So Sanh Mo Hinh

Cac backbone duoc su dung: ResNet50, EfficientNet-B6, ViT-B16. Qua 2 mode du lieu (original/augmentation) va 3 chien luoc fine-tuning (frozen, linear probe, finetune all), tong cong 15 run.

Training engine dung CrossEntropyLoss (co label smoothing), optimizer AdamW, scheduler CosineAnnealingLR, early stopping, va ghi log TensorBoard theo epoch/batch.
